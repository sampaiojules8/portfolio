I’m Jules, a product designer at Credit Genie and the design lead on Monyo.

I’m originally from Brazil. I studied Graphic Design at SCAD, with a minor in Interaction Design and study abroad in France and Hong Kong.

I spent more than five years at Attentive, growing from visual designer to team lead and design manager of a team of six, then to product designer leading end-to-end design for the Creative Builder. In each role, I’ve paired visual craft with a user-centered approach to product.

At Credit Genie, I built the company’s design system from scratch, and I’m now building Monyo’s, taking each from Figma tokens to components merged into production code. I also design product areas used by hundreds of thousands of members on iOS and Android.

I’m an aspiring design engineer and contribute code to our iOS repository. I bridge design and engineering through direct, honest collaboration.

Outside of work, I’m usually trying new restaurants, browsing thrift stores, or spending time with my Italian greyhound.
